PRO SBORKE V F:\pc_app (v3.4)

1. Skachajte NOVYJ arhiv vsy.888.zip (ssylka v chate ili na sayte).
2. Raspekujte i ZAMENITE vsje soderzhimoye papki pc_app v F:\pc_app
   (vajno zamenit', chtoby novye build_exe.bat i start.bat).
3. Dvoynoy klik start.bat - okno ostanetsya otkrytym i pokaet rezultat.
   Esli GUI ne podnyalsya - smotrite log_start.txt i nybanner_error.log.
4. Dlya sborki EXE: dvoynoy klik build_exe.bat. Vsyo pishetsya v
   log_build.txt s otmetkami vremeni. Esli ostanovilos na "Sobirayu EXE"
   boleye 5 minut - otkroyte log_build.txt: skoreye vsego Windows Defender
   blokiruyet PyInstaller. Dobavte papku F:\pc_app v isklyucheniya
   (Bezopasnost Windows -> Osnovnye parametry -> Isklyucheniya)
   i zapustite batnik zanovo.

Chto ispravleno v etoy versii:
- Ubrana zagruzka pillow/PIL (lagala pri ustanovke i ne nuzhna).
- PyInstaller vazyvaetsya cherez "python -m PyInstaller" (PATH ne vajen).
- V log pishutsya vremennyye metki nachala i kontsa sborki.
- Okna batnikov nikogda ne zakryvayutsya sami (pause vsegda v kontse).
